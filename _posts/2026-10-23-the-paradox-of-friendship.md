What does it take to make a friend? 

I once watched an interview with Emma Watson, on being "friends" with her costars (you can see the interview [here](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/YPeJb2xQuTc)- the video I found that clips the exact quote is a youtube short). Her response was that, although actors want to be friends and there is media pressure to play up their friendship, the truth is that being friends takes energy and input that the cast simply cannot provide.

In particular, it takes **time**, **trust**, and **presence**. Three investments that must be made and maintained, for which the return is genuine friendship.
