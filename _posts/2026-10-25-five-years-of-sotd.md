It has been 5 years of sotd!

Things to talk about

I really like music

It means a lot to me, no emotional talent but music enjoyer

First year wrapped came out in 2022 I was brought to tears because big year for me summarized

This has a similar sentiment. All of my highschool (since it lined up with getting spotify), all of my uni, every song i liked deliberately is here. Every day that anything has happened, the best days, worst days, dates, days i was really sick, sat an exam, cried alone, made a new friend, went on any trip, stayed up late texting someone, woke up early to run, went to a party, every memory from the last 4 years. All the emotion of my life.

Analysis of the whole thing is unnatural, like looking into my soul. Every song entry has come from a place of emotion, connection, feeling, soul. So to abstract that away and analyse trends is so invasive and robotic and without feeling, because how can you strip away that part of the song which is so important and turn it into a number. Has the same feeling as reducing lives to statistics in a war. 

didnt want to do it. but ultimately curiosity got the best of me. Lets see what trends we can identify.
